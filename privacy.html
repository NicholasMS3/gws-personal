Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This app is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own Google Tasks and Google Calendar.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the app reads and changes Google Tasks and Google Calendar data in the owner's Google account.

How data is used and stored

Data goes directly between Google's servers and the owner's own computer. Login credentials are stored encrypted on that computer. The app runs no server of its own and does not collect, store, sell or share data with any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
